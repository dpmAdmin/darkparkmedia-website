Process

How a shoot runs

Scope & scout

We define the deliverables, walk or review the location, and plan coverage — including whether Bay Aerials joins the shoot for aerial passes.

Shoot day

A tight crew with a shot list. Ground cinematography, photography, and any coordinated aerial work captured in one scheduled pass.

Edit & finish

Selects, cut, color, and sound handled in-house. You review online; revisions are structured and scheduled, not open-ended.

Delivery

Final masters delivered in every format the project needs — and if Motion Marketing is engaged, the work goes straight into distribution.
