The people

Small crew.
Full pipeline.

Dark Park Media runs as one coordinated team out of Northern California. The person flying the drone talks to the person cutting the edit. The person cutting the edit talks to the person scheduling the posts. Nothing gets lost in a handoff, because there isn't one.

On any given week that means prepping cameras for an architectural shoot, reviewing aerial passes from a survey flight, finishing a commercial edit, and loading a month of content into a client's channels — the same people, moving the same projects, from field to feed.

When a job needs more hands, we scale the crew. When it needs one specialist, you get exactly that — without briefing three separate vendors.
