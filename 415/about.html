About

The studio behind the listings

Four One Five Visuals is the real estate media brand of Dark Park Media LLC, working with listing agents across Marin County and San Francisco. We photograph, film and fly the listing, and deliver it through one portal: photos within 24 hours, films within 5 days.

  • 20+ yearsin television editorial behind the films
  • 24 hoursfrom shoot to delivered photos
  • FAA Part 107certified drone operations
  • Marin + SFMill Valley to the city, Sonoma and the East Bay by arrangement

Who you're working with

Four One Five Visuals was founded by Anthony Slezak, who spent two decades cutting television before turning the same craft on real estate: lead editor on Discovery's Street Outlaws from 2017 to 2024 and supervising editor on its live and YouTube side since, with earlier credits on American Chopper, Dirty Jobs and UFC's The Ultimate Fighter. That background is why our listing films are cut to the pace people actually watch at, and why every film ships branded and unbranded with vertical cuts for social.

How we work

  • Photography: interiors bracketed and hand-finished with true colors. We never change the color of a material. Exteriors are timed to when the light suits the front of the house, with real or virtual twilight available.
  • Film: 60 to 120 second listing films with aerial openings and twilight endings, cut with broadcast pacing and delivered branded and unbranded.
  • Aerials: drone photos up to 100 megapixels and 6K video, flown under FAA Part 107, for lot lines, views and neighborhood context.
  • Marin light: hillsides, redwoods and canyons mean the sun clears the ridge later than the clock says. We plan every shoot around when the light actually reaches the house.
  • Booking: price, date and payment in one place in the portal. Prices step with the home's size and show before you commit. Pay when you book or after delivery.

The wider studio

Dark Park Media also runs Bay Aerials for aerial cinematography and data, and Motion Marketing for commercial and branded video. Four One Five Visuals is the part of the studio built for listings.
