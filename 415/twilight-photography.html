Twilight photography for Marin listings

The twilight frame is the one that stops the scroll on the MLS: warm windows, a deep blue sky, the house glowing from inside. We shoot it as a return visit at dusk, timed to the sunset for your date and to when the light actually leaves a Marin hillside.

What you get

  • Real twilight session. A dusk visit timed to the sunset, with a tight set of five to eight finished frames of the strongest angles: the front elevation, the pool and outdoor rooms, delivered within 24 hours at MLS and print sizes. The usable window is about fifteen minutes, so we plan the angles in advance and shoot them in order.
  • Premium twilight. Blended, hand-finished frames that hold the sky and the interiors together, plus a twilight aerial when conditions allow the drone to fly.
  • Virtual twilight. A daytime exterior converted in post, sold per image and delivered labeled as virtual. Three are included with every Launch package.

Real ground twilight is part of the Showcase package and above, and can be added to any photo shoot or booked on its own. Prices step with the home's size and show in the booking portal before you commit.

Questions

When exactly is twilight shot?

In the 20 to 30 minutes after sunset, when the sky still holds color and the house lights read warm without blowing out. We schedule to the actual sunset for the date, and on Marin hillsides to when the ridge actually takes the light.

What should be ready before a twilight shoot?

Every interior and exterior light on, including landscape, pool and pathway lighting; blinds open; cars out of the driveway and off the street in front. We arrive before sunset to set up and check sightlines.

Real twilight or virtual twilight?

Real twilight is a return visit at dusk and gives you the sky, the glow and the reflections as they were. Virtual twilight is a daytime exterior converted in post: faster and cheaper, and we label it as such. For a hero frame, real twilight; for secondary exteriors or a quick launch, virtual.

What if fog or wind rolls in?

Twilight and drone sessions reschedule free of charge for weather. We watch the forecast and will suggest a different evening rather than deliver a grey sky.
