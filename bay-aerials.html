Bay
Aerials

Perspective, data, and cinematography from above. A Part 107-compliant drone operation flying for film sets, photographers, surveyors, builders, and anyone who needs the view - or the data - from altitude.

Services

What we fly

Aerial Cinematography

Planned, repeatable cinematic drone moves for films, commercials, and property work - flown to a shot list, not improvised on site.

Drone B-Roll

Establishing shots, reveals, and coverage passes delivered as graded or camera-original files, ready to cut into your edit.

Aerial Photography

High-resolution stills from altitude for listings, construction milestones, marketing, and documentation.

Scans & Mapping

Orthomosaic maps and 3D scans of sites, structures, and terrain, processed into usable deliverables.

Survey Support

Aerial data capture flown in support of survey and engineering workflows, coordinated with your team's requirements.

Site Documentation

Scheduled flight passes that document progress on construction and development sites over time.

All flights are conducted under FAA Part 107 rules, with airspace authorization handled before the crew arrives on site.

Process

How a flight runs

Mission planning

We confirm the deliverable - film, stills, scan, or survey data - check airspace, secure any required authorizations, and plan the flight.

Flight day

A licensed remote pilot flies the planned mission. Cinematic work is flown to a shot list; data work is flown to a capture grid.

Processing

Footage is graded and delivered; scan and mapping data is processed into orthomosaics, models, or the format your workflow needs.

Handoff or pipeline

Files go straight to you - or into the Dark Park Media pipeline, where Four One Five Visuals cuts them in and Motion Marketing distributes the result.
